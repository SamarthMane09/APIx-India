Econometric Methodology & Weighting

1. The Axiomatic Jevons Geometric Mean

Standard arithmetic means (such as the Carli index) suffer from upward substitution bias. Following IMF/ILO Consumer Price Index standards, APIx-India aggregates route-level flight price relatives using the Jevons index:

I_route = [ ∏_{i=1}^N ( P_{i,t} / P_{i,0} ) ]^(1/N) × 100

Computed in log-space: exp( (1/N) * Σ ln(P_t / P_0) ) * 100 to guarantee numerical precision.

2. DGCA Passenger Traffic Weighting

Elementary route indices are aggregated into a National Airfare Price Index using Directorate General of Civil Aviation quarterly passenger traffic volume weights:

I_National = Σ ( w_route × I_route )
