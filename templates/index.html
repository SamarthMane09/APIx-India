Econometric Methodology

1. Jevons Geometric Mean Index

In compliance with IMF and ILO Consumer Price Index guidelines, route price relatives are aggregated with the Jevons elementary formula to eliminate upward substitution bias:

I_route = [ ∏ ( P_t / P_0 ) ]^(1/N) × 100

2. DGCA Passenger Traffic Weights

National index aggregates trunk routes using DGCA quarterly volume weights:

DEL-BOM 48%
BLR-DEL 32%
BOM-BLR 20%

3. Advance Lead-Time Windows (T+1 to T+45)

Distinguishes dynamic last-minute yield surges from underlying ticket price inflation.
